# GZ-0002: radiation reinjection reset occurs during packing

- Status: confirmed
- Confidence: high
- Affected flags: `RT_SOURCE_INJECTION`, `RT_REINJECT_ACCRETED_PHOTONS`
- Oracle: invariant (property)

## Evidence

The communication input function copies `BH_accreted_photon_energy` into the outgoing luminosity and immediately zeros the particle field. Packing is earlier than successful application and can participate in exchange retry paths.

Pinned source: [destructive reset in input packing](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/radiation/rt_source_injection.c#L65-L68).

## Reproduce or falsify

Pack a source with nonzero stored photon energy, then abandon or retry before evaluation; the source has already been cleared. Falsify by proving packing is exactly once and cannot precede an abandoned interaction.

## Impact

Accreted photon energy may be lost or inconsistently reinjected.
