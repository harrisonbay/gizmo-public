# GZ-0001: adaptive tree-force decision can skip `All.G`

- Status: confirmed
- Confidence: high
- Affected flags: `ADAPTIVE_TREEFORCE_UPDATE`
- Oracle: invariant (property)

## Evidence

Particle selection calls `needs_new_treeforce()` before the tree walk, while final processing calls it again. The walk can update predicate inputs such as `min_bh_fb_time`. A true-to-false flip takes the jerk-only `continue` before `GravAccel` is multiplied by `All.G`; `GravJerk` is converted later as well.

Pinned source: [post-walk decision and conversion](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/gravity/gravtree.c#L497-L508), [pre-walk selection](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/gravity/gravtree.c#L710-L715), [walk mutation](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/gravity/forcetree.c#L2818-L2820).

## Reproduce or falsify

Instrument both decisions and force a walk to move `min_bh_fb_time` across the threshold. Reproduction is a true pre-walk decision followed by false post-walk and the early `continue`. Falsify by proving all predicate inputs immutable for the full interval.

## Impact

An active particle can retain acceleration and jerk in inconsistent units, perturbing its dynamics.
