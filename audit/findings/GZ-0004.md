# GZ-0004: possible SIDM/OpenMP RNG race

- Status: under investigation
- Confidence: medium
- Affected flags: `DM_SIDM`, `_OPENMP`
- Oracle: concurrency stress (dynamic)

## Evidence

The SIDM pair kernel samples `gsl_rng_uniform(random_generator)`. Nearby shared particle writes are atomic, but the call shows no lock or per-thread RNG selection. The kernel is included by the threaded adaptive-softening loop.

Pinned source: [SIDM sampling and atomic writes](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/sidm/sidm_core_flux_computation.h#L1-L34), [kernel inclusion](https://github.com/pfhopkins/gizmo-public/blob/a828c4ba79d67093bd1a3d04f6f90b5d2d94d65f/gravity/ags_hsml.c#L907).

## Reproduce or falsify

Run a collision-rich seeded case with multiple OpenMP threads under ThreadSanitizer and compare interaction statistics with one thread. Falsify by proving the RNG object is thread-local here or by a covered, clean forced-concurrency run.

## Impact

If shared, RNG state races could corrupt or bias sampled SIDM interactions. This remains a hypothesis until ownership or runtime evidence resolves it.
